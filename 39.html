Biology • Cell Life Cycle

Cells Grow, Stop & Renew

Cells follow controlled patterns of growth and replacement. In animal tissues, contact with neighbouring cells can act as a signal to stop division.

Block 39: Cell Growth, Death, and Contact Inhibition

↻

Cell Life Span

Every cell has a definite life span; dead cells are replaced by new ones.

⏸

Contact Inhibition

Cell division stops when animal cells contact neighbouring cells.

!

Cancer Cells

Cancer cells lose contact inhibition control, divide uncontrollably, and form tumours.

🌱

Plant Cells

Due to rigid cell walls, plant cells do not show contact inhibition and follow a different growth pattern.
