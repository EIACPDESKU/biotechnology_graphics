Electron Microscope

When light is no longer enough, electrons reveal a much smaller world — allowing scientists to explore cellular structures at the nanometre scale.

Block 12: Electron Microscope

  • Uses a beam of electrons instead of light.
  • Reveals cell structure at the nanometre (nm) scale.
  • 1 nanometre = one-billionth of a metre.
  • Provides much higher resolution than light microscopes.
