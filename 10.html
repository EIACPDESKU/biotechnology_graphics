Total Magnification

A microscope combines the power of its eyepiece and objective lens to make a tiny cell appear much larger.

Block 10: Total Magnification of a Microscope

Total magnification = Eyepiece power × Objective lens power
  • Example: Eyepiece (10X) × Objective lens (10X) = 100X
  • A cell of estimated size 200 µm will appear 100 times larger.
