Improvements in Microscopy

Modern microscopes do more than simply enlarge an image. Better microscopy combines magnification, resolution and contrast to reveal increasingly clear details.

Block 11: Improvements in Microscopy

Three main features improved in microscopes:

  • Resolution — measure of clarity
  • Contrast — difference in brightness between parts
  • Magnification — enlargement of the image
