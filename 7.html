Cell Biology • 1665

Robert Hooke & the Discovery of Cells

In 1665, Robert Hooke looked through a microscope at a thin slice of cork and discovered tiny box-like compartments that he called “cells”.

Block 7: Robert Hooke and the Discovery of Cells

  • Robert Hooke first observed a cell in 1665.
  • Used a self-designed microscope (200–300X magnification).
  • Examined a thin slice of cork → observed box-like compartments.
  • Named them 'cells'.
