Block 45: Chapter Summary — At a Glance

The Cell — Basic Unit of Life

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms. Its structure, organelles, genetic material, and ability to divide together explain how living systems function.

C

Cell: Basic Unit

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms.

P

Prokaryotic Cells

No well-defined nucleus; genetic material lies in a nucleoid. No membrane-bound organelles.

E

Eukaryotic Cells

Larger and more complex, with a well-defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.

M

Membrane & Cell Wall

All cells have a cell membrane. Plants, fungi, and bacteria also have a cell wall.

DNA

Nucleus & Genetic Information

The nucleus contains chromosomes made of DNA + proteins, carrying genetic information.

O

Key Organelles

Nucleus, ER, mitochondria, Golgi apparatus, ribosomes, and lysosomes perform specialised cellular functions.

🌿

Plant-Specific Plastids

Plastids include chloroplasts, leucoplasts, and chromoplasts.

÷

Cell Division

Mitosis forms 2 identical daughter cells, while meiosis forms 4 cells with half the chromosome number.

Mitosis vs Meiosis — Remember the Numbers

Mitosis 1 division → 2 genetically identical cells
→
Meiosis 2 divisions → 4 cells with half chromosomes
Growth & Cancer: Normal cells grow and die in a controlled manner. Cancer cells lose this control and divide uncontrollably, which can lead to the formation of tumours.
