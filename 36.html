Biology • Cell Division

Mitosis
vs Meiosis

Both are forms of cell division, but they serve very different purposes — one maintains body cells, while the other creates gametes for sexual reproduction.

Block 36: Mitosis vs Meiosis — Comparison

Feature Mitosis Meiosis
Number of divisions 1 2
Daughter cells produced 2 4
Genetic identity Identical to parent Half the chromosomes; genetically varied
Purpose Growth, repair, asexual reproduction Sexual reproduction, genetic diversity
Occurs in Body cells Reproductive organ cells
