Cells to Organ Systems

Living organisms are built through levels of organization: cells combine to form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs cooperate as organ systems.

Block 4: Cells to Organ Systems

  • Similar cells performing similar functions → tissue
  • Different tissues organize → organ
  • Several organs work together → organ system
  • Example: nasal pores + nasal cavity + trachea + lungs = respiratory system
