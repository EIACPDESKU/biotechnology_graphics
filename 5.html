Limit of Resolution

The human eye has a finite ability to distinguish two nearby points. When they become too close, they appear as one.

Block 5: Limit of Resolution

  • Limit of resolution: ability to see two close points as separate and distinct.
  • Measured from 25 cm (near point of human eye).
  • Human eye's limit of resolution = 0.1 mm.
