Nucleus

The nucleus is the cell's information center — storing DNA, organizing genetic instructions, and coordinating the activities of the cell.

Block 22: Nucleus — House of Coded Instructions

  • Nuclear membrane: double-layered, has nuclear pores for material transfer between nucleus and cytoplasm.
  • Nucleolus: dense round body; site of ribosomal subunit synthesis.
  • Chromatin: entangled thread-like DNA mass in non-dividing cells.
  • Chromosomes: rod-shaped structures; chromatin organizes into these when the cell is about to divide.
  • Chromosomes = DNA + proteins; contain genes (functional DNA segments) carrying genetic information.
