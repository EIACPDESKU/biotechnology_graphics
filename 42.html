One Cell, A Whole Plant

A mature plant cell can retain the remarkable potential to rebuild an entire plant when given the right nutrients and environmental conditions.

Block 42: Totipotency and Plant Tissue Culture

H

Gottlieb Haberlandt — 1902

The Austrian botanist proposed that a living plant cell could have the potential to develop into a complete plant under suitable conditions.

1

Totipotency

Any living plant cell, even a mature cell, can develop into a complete plant when provided with suitable nutrients and environmental conditions.

↗

A Foundation for Biotechnology

Haberlandt's idea laid the foundation for Plant Tissue Culture Technology, allowing plants to be regenerated from cells or tissues under controlled conditions.

Key Idea

Living plant cell → suitable conditions → complete plant
