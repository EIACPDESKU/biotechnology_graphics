Evolutionary Link

Mitochondria and plastids carry their own DNA and ribosomes — clues that point toward an ancient bacterial partnership inside early cells.

Block 30: Mitochondria & Plastids — Evolutionary Link

  • Both mitochondria and plastids have:
  • Their own DNA.
  • Their own ribosomes.
  • Ability to make some of their own proteins.
  • This suggests they share an evolutionary history with single-celled bacteria (endosymbiotic origin).
