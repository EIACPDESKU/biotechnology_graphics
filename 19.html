Biology • Osmosis

Wall vs Membrane

In a concentrated solution, water leaves the cell. A cell wall can preserve the outer shape of a plant cell, while an animal cell has no such rigid support.

Block 19: Cell Wall vs Cell Membrane in Osmosis

  • In concentrated sugar solution:
    • Plant cells: lose water via osmosis, but cell wall maintains shape; only inner content (cell membrane) shrinks away from wall.
    • Animal cells (e.g., cheek cells): no cell wall → shrink considerably as they lose water.
Conclusion: Cell wall keeps plant cells firm in original shape; lack of cell wall lets animal cells change shape easily.
