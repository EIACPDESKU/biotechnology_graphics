Unicellular vs Multicellular

Life can exist as a single independent cell or as a complex community of cells working together as one organism.

Block 3: Unicellular vs Multicellular Organisms

  • All living organisms are made up of cells.
  • Unicellular: one cell only (e.g., bacteria, yeast).
  • Multicellular: millions of cells working together (e.g., plants, fish, humans).
