Mitochondria

The powerhouse of the cell — converting the chemical energy stored in glucose into ATP, the cell's usable energy currency.

Block 28: Mitochondria — Powerhouse of the Cell

  • Called the 'powerhouse of the cell' — supplies energy for cellular activities.
  • Structure (two membranes):
    • Outer membrane: smooth and porous.
    • Inner membrane: folded into cristae (finger-like projections) → increases surface area for reactions.
  • Process: cellular respiration — breaks down glucose to release energy.
  • Energy stored as: ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) — the "energy currency" of the cell.
