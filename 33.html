Cell
Division

One pre-existing cell gives rise to new cells, allowing living organisms to grow, repair, maintain tissues, and reproduce.

Block 33: Cell Division — Overview

  • Cell division: process forming new cells from pre-existing cells.
  • Purpose: growth, repair of damaged tissues, reproduction.
  • Two major types:
    Type Purpose
    Mitosis Normal growth, repair, maintenance, asexual reproduction
    Meiosis Sexual reproduction, creates genetic diversity
