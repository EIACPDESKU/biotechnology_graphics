Endoplasmic Reticulum

A vast membrane network inside the cell that acts like a production and transport system for proteins, lipids, and hormones.

Block 25: Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)

  • ER: large network-like organelle in cytoplasm; continuous with the nuclear envelope.
  • Role: synthesis and transport of proteins, fats (lipids), and hormones.
Type Ribosomes Function
Rough ER (RER) Present (looks rough) Protein synthesis & secretion (e.g., pancreatic cells)
Smooth ER (SER) Absent (looks smooth) Synthesis & storage of fats and hormones
