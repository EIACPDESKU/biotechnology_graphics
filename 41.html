Biology • Cell Regulation

When Growth Loses Control

Healthy cells grow, age, and die in an organised manner. When this control system breaks down, abnormal cells can multiply continuously and form tumours.

Block 41: Cancer — Tumours

✓

Normal Cell Behaviour

Normal cells grow, age, and die in a controlled manner.

!

Loss of Control

When this control system breaks down, abnormal cells grow and divide uncontrollably.

Benign Tumour

A non-invasive tumour that does not invade nearby tissues.

Malignant Tumour

A cancerous tumour that can invade nearby tissues and spread to other parts of the body, forming new tumours.
