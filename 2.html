First Protective Membrane

Mineral deposits around ancient hot springs may have created sheltered environments where early organic molecules could survive.

Block 2: First Protective Membrane

  • Calcium carbonate deposits formed rapidly around hot springs.
  • These deposits protected early organic molecules from radiation and harsh conditions.
  • They may have helped form the first protective membrane — the barrier that defines a cell.
