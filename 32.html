Biology • Cell Storage

Vacuoles

Cellular storage spaces that hold water and dissolved substances while helping plant cells stay firm and upright.

Block 32: Vacuoles — Storage and Support

  • Plant cells: one large central vacuole, surrounded by a selectively permeable membrane.
  • Filled with watery fluid: cell sap (stores water, minerals, sugars, waste).
  • Maintains pressure/firmness in the cell.
  • Lack of water → vacuole loses water → cell less firm → plant wilts.
  • Animal cells: smaller vacuoles, used for temporary storage only.
