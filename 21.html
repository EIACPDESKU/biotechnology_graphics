Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic

Two fundamental types of cells — one with a membrane-bound nucleus and one whose DNA remains in an unenclosed nucleoid region.

Block 21: Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic Cells

Characteristic Prokaryotic Cell Eukaryotic Cell
Primitive nucleus (nucleoid) Present Absent
Diameter of typical cell 1–10 µm 10–100 µm
Number of cells in organism Usually unicellular Unicellular or multicellular
Membrane-bound organelles Absent Present
Membrane-bound nucleus Absent Present
  • Pro = primitive, karyon = nucleus
  • Eu = true, karyon = nucleus
