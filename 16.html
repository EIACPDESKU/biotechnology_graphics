What Happens to a Cell?

The concentration of solute outside a cell determines the direction of water movement and whether the cell stays unchanged, swells, or shrinks.

Block 16: Isotonic, Hypotonic, Hypertonic Solutions

Condition Solution Type Effect on Cell
Extracellular solute = Intracellular solute Isotonic No change
Extracellular solute < Intracellular solute Hypotonic Cell swells
Extracellular solute > Intracellular solute Hypertonic Cell shrinks
