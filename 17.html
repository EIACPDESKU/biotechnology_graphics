The Fluid-Mosaic Model

A thin, flexible membrane made from a moving lipid bilayer with proteins embedded throughout — like a dynamic molecular mosaic.

Block 17: Fluid-Mosaic Model of Cell Membrane

  • Cell membrane thickness: 7–10 nm (1 nm = 0.000001 mm)
  • Composed of: lipids and proteins
  • Fluid-Mosaic Model:
    • Lipid bilayer: two layers of fat molecules (water-attracting heads outward, water-repelling tails inward)
    • Molecules can move sideways, flip, rotate → membrane is fluid
    • Proteins act as gatekeepers for substance passage
    • Arrangement like tiles → called 'mosaic'
