Three Parts of a Cell

Every cell has three fundamental components: a protective membrane, a living cytoplasm, and a nucleus that acts as the control center.

Block 20: Three Basic Parts of a Cell

Three basic parts of a cell:

  1. Plasma membrane — selectively permeable boundary
  2. Cytoplasm — semi-fluid, jelly-like substance
  3. Nucleus — prominent control center
  • Cytoplasm contains sub-cellular components called organelles.
