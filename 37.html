Biology • Cell Division

When Cell Division Goes Wrong

Errors during mitosis or meiosis can disturb normal cell behaviour, chromosome number, development, and reproduction.

Block 37: Errors in Mitosis and Meiosis

M Errors in Mitosis

  • Leads to uncontrolled cell division.
  • Causes tumours and abnormal chromosome numbers.

μ Errors in Meiosis

  • May result in genetic disorders.
  • Linked to developmental problems or distinctive physical features.
  • Can cause early pregnancy loss or reduced fertility.
