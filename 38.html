The Cell Theory

Three scientists helped establish one of biology's most fundamental ideas: every living thing is built from cells, and every new cell comes from another cell.

Block 38: Cell Theory

Classical Cell Theory

1838

Matthias Schleiden

All plants are made of cells.

1839

Theodor Schwann

All animals are made of cells.

1855

Rudolf Virchow

New cells form only from pre-existing cells.

Cell Theory Principles

  1. All living organisms are made up of one or more cells.
  2. The cell is the basic unit of structure and function in living beings.
  3. All cells arise from pre-existing cells.
