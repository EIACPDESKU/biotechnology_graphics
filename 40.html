Biology • Cell Regulation

Death by Design

Programmed cell death is not accidental destruction. It is a genetically regulated process that removes selected cells when the body needs them to disappear.

Block 40: Programmed Cell Death (PCD)

PCD: genetically regulated, organised process of selective cell destruction.

⌁

Normal Development

Removes selected cells to shape developing tissues correctly.

✓

Cellular Quality Control

Eliminates cells that are no longer needed or should be removed.

+

Immune Function

Helps regulate and maintain healthy cell populations.

✦

Example: Developing Hands

During embryo development, PCD eliminates cells between the digits. This separation prevents the formation of webbed hands.
