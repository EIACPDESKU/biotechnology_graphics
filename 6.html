Biology • Microscopy

Magnification and Microscopes

Optical lenses enlarge tiny objects, allowing structures such as cells to become visible when they are too small for the unaided human eye.

Block 6: Magnification and Microscopes

  • Convex lens or combination of lenses used for magnification.
  • A microscope uses an objective lens and an eyepiece.
  • Cells are usually too small to be seen by the unaided eye.
