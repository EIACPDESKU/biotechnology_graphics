Lysosomes

The cell's clean-up and recycling system — breaking down unwanted materials and returning useful components to the cytoplasm.

Block 27: Lysosomes — The Clean-up System

  • Lysosomes: single membrane-bound sacs filled with enzymes.
  • Function: break down unwanted proteins, carbohydrates, fats, and damaged organelles.
  • Breakdown products are released into the cytoplasm for reuse.
  • Special fact: Human sperm cells contain lysosomal enzymes that help break down the egg's outer layer during fertilisation.
