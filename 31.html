Chromoplasts
& Leucoplasts

Plastids can specialize either in displaying colourful pigments or in quietly storing the food a plant needs.

Block 31: Chromoplasts and Leucoplasts

  • Chromoplasts (Greek: chroma = colour):
    • Contain pigments other than chlorophyll (yellow, orange, red).
    • Found in flower petals and fruits.
    • Attract pollinators and fruit-eating animals (seed dispersal).
  • Leucoplasts (Greek: leukos = white):
    • Colorless plastids.
    • Store food material: starch, oils, or proteins.
    • Example: leucoplasts in potato and taro (Colocasia) store starch.
