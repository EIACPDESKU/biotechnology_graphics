Osmosis vs Diffusion

Both processes involve movement from one region to another, but osmosis specifically describes the movement of water through a selectively permeable membrane.

Block 15: Osmosis vs Diffusion

  • Diffusion: net movement of particles from higher → lower concentration (no membrane needed).
  • Osmosis: diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane.
  • Water moves from dilute (more water, less solute) → concentrated (less water, more solute) solution.
  • In plants, osmosis helps water enter root cells from soil.
