Nucleoid & RBC Exception

One cell stores its DNA without a nucleus. Another deliberately loses its nucleus to make more room for oxygen-carrying haemoglobin.

Block 23: Prokaryotic Nucleoid & RBC Exception

  • Nucleoid: region in prokaryotic cells containing genetic material (single circular DNA), with no membrane around it.
  • Exception: Mature Red Blood Cells (RBCs) in humans are enucleate (no nucleus).
    • More space for haemoglobin → transports more oxygen.
    • Cannot repair or divide themselves.
    • Lifespan: approximately 120 days.
