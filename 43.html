Cells Under Culture

Cell culture allows living plant or animal cells to grow outside the body in a carefully controlled, nutrient-rich environment.

Block 43: Cell Culture

What is Cell Culture?

Cell culture is the process of growing plant or animal cells outside the body under specially controlled conditions.

N

Nutrient-rich Medium

Cells are placed in a medium containing the nutrients required for growth and survival.

T

Temperature

The environment is maintained at the appropriate temperature for healthy cell growth.

pH

Acidic / Alkaline Balance

The chemical environment is carefully controlled to maintain suitable acidic or alkaline conditions.

✓

Moisture & Sterility

Adequate moisture is maintained while contamination is prevented through sterile conditions.

Applications of Cell Culture

  • Studying how cells work
  • Producing biochemicals
  • Food production
  • Medicine production
  • Vaccine production
