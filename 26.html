Biology • Cell Packaging

Golgi Apparatus

The cell's packaging and distribution centre — modifying, sorting, and sending cellular products to their destinations.

Block 26: Golgi Apparatus — Packaging Centre

  • Structure: stacks of flattened, sac-like structures.
  • Functionally linked to ER, cell membrane, and other organelles.
  • Function: acts like the cell's post office:
    • Modifies, sorts, and packages proteins and/or lipids.
    • Forms vesicles for transport, secretion, or lysosome formation.
  • Discovered by Camillo Golgi (1898) in nerve cells of a barn owl.
