Plastids

Specialized plant-cell organelles that capture sunlight, synthesize food, and store the products of photosynthesis.

Block 29: Plastids — Food Synthesis Organelles

  • Plastids: special plant cell organelles for food synthesis & storage.
  • Chloroplasts:
    • Double-membrane-bound (like mitochondria).
    • Contain chlorophyll (green pigment) — absorbs sunlight for photosynthesis.
    • Inner semi-fluid substance: stroma.
    • Disc-shaped membrane structures within stroma contain chlorophyll.
    • Sugars synthesized are stored in stroma along with starch granules.
