Meiosis

A specialised cell division that reduces the chromosome number by half and produces four genetically distinct gametes.

Block 35: Meiosis

  • Meiosis: produces gametes; occurs only in reproductive organ cells.
  • Location:

    Animals: testes (sperm), ovaries (eggs)

    Plants: anthers (pollen), ovaries (egg cells)

  • Process:
    • Parent cell divides twice → forms 4 daughter cells.
    • 1st division: chromosome number reduced to half.
    • 2nd division: similar to mitosis (splits again).
    • Each gamete has half the DNA of parent cell.
    • Fertilisation restores original chromosome number.
