The Cell Wall

A strong outer covering that gives plant cells structural support while remaining permeable to water and dissolved minerals.

Block 18: Cell Wall — Outer Covering

  • Present in: plant, fungal, and bacterial cells (absent in animal cells).
  • Provides rigid structural support; helps withstand wind/rain.
  • Helps leaves/flowers stay firm and plants stay upright.
  • Permeable: allows water and dissolved minerals to pass through.
  • Made primarily of cellulose (a carbohydrate) in plants.
