Potato Osmosis Experiment

A simple potato experiment demonstrates how water moves across cell membranes and how the surrounding solution affects the size and weight of potato tissue.

Block 14: Osmosis Experiment (Potato Activity)

Experimental Setup:

  • Beaker A: potato in plain water
  • Beaker B: potato in 20% salt/sugar solution

Observations:

  • Beaker A → potato piece swells (weight increases)
  • Beaker B → potato piece shrinks (weight decreases)
