Origin of Life

Exploring the watery environments and extreme conditions that may have provided a starting point for life on Earth.

Block 1: Origin of Life

  • Life originated in water, possibly in small water pools with changing conditions.
  • Hot springs (e.g., Puga Valley, Ladakh) resemble early Earth conditions (~3.5 billion years ago).
  • Host organisms: thermophiles — heat-loving, unicellular bacteria.
