Biology • Cell Division

Mitosis

One parent cell divides to produce two genetically identical daughter cells, preserving the genetic information of the body.

Block 34: Mitosis

  • Mitosis: most common type of cell division.
  • Produces two genetically identical daughter cells from one parent cell.
  • Each daughter cell has:
    • Same DNA
    • Same number of chromosomes as parent
  • Ensures genetic information is maintained across body cells.
